The kitchen behind the cakes

I started baking at home in KR Puram in 2019, for my cousin’s birthday. My mother-in-law tasted the first sponge, said it needed less sugar, and she was right. We still bake by that rule.

Neighbours began ordering for their own families, then friends of friends. Today the same small kitchen on 16th Cross handles weddings and Sunday birthdays alike, and I check every cake before it leaves.

If something is not right, message me directly. I would rather fix it than lose a customer.
